Sawaal

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Hamara kaam register aur Excel se chal raha hai. Badalna kyun?

Register mein time koi bhi likh sakta hai aur leave ka record WhatsApp mein kho jaata hai. Month end pe salary banane mein sabse zyada time yahi jaata hai. DUNE mein ye sab apne aap record hota hai, aur hisaab ready milta hai.

Hamara office sirf 10–15 logon ka hai. Kya ye zyada nahi hai?

Nahi. DUNE chhote offices ke liye hi bana hai. Setup kuch minute ka hai aur staff ko koi training nahi chahiye.

Setup mein kitna time lagta hai?

Account 2 minute mein ban jaata hai. Staff add karna aur office timing set karna lagbhag 10 minute ka kaam hai. Agle din se attendance app pe lag sakti hai.

Remote ya work from home staff ka kya?

Remote staff bhi app se check-in kar sakta hai, aur WFH leave type alag se track hota hai. Office aur remote dono ek hi dashboard pe dikhte hain.

Staff office ke bahar se check-in toh nahi kar lega?

Geo-fencing on karo, toh check-in sirf office location ke andar se hi hoga.

Paise dene se pehle try kar sakte hain?

Haan. Free trial mein credit card nahi chahiye. Apni team ke saath try karo, pasand aaye tabhi aage badho.
