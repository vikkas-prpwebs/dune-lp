Frequently asked questions

We manage fine with a register and Excel. Why switch?

Anyone can write any time in a register, and leave records get lost in WhatsApp. That's where most of the time goes when you make salaries at month end. DUNE records all of it automatically, so the numbers are ready.

Our office has only 10–15 people. Isn't this too much?

No. DUNE is built for small offices. Setup takes a few minutes and your staff need no training.

How long does setup take?

Your account is ready in about 2 minutes. Adding staff and setting office hours takes around 10 minutes. You can take attendance in the app from the next day.

What about remote or work-from-home staff?

Remote staff can check in from the app too, and WFH is tracked as its own leave type. Office and remote staff show up on the same dashboard.

Can staff check in from outside the office?

Turn on geo-fencing and check-in only works inside your office location.

Can we try it before paying?

Yes. The free trial needs no credit card. Try it with your team and continue only if you like it.
